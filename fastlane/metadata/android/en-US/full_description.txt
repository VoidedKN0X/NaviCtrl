<p>A full music player for self-hosted Navidrome servers.</p>
<ul>
<li>Songs tab with your whole library, A to Z or by date added</li>
<li>Shuffle all: an endless random queue</li>
<li>Favourites tab for songs, albums and artists</li>
<li>Background playback with lock screen and Bluetooth controls</li>
<li>Synced lyrics, sleep timer and mixes of similar songs</li>
<li>Reorderable up-next queue that survives a restart</li>
<li>Downloads for offline listening and a cache of played songs</li>
<li>Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz through Navidrome</li>
<li>Playlists tab: play, shuffle, create, rename, delete and edit your Navidrome playlists</li>
<li>Album and artist pages and search</li>
<li>Several servers and accounts; separate Wi-Fi and mobile data quality</li>
</ul>
<p>Google-free and de-Googled: no Google Play services, Firebase or other Google services, and the app never contacts Google. It works the same on de-Googled phones (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS without Google apps).</p>
<p>Telemetry-free: no analytics, crash reporting, usage stats or tracking of any kind.</p>
